DigiVasool

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

DigiVasool helps small lenders (“organisations”) record loans, collections and borrowers. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

Who is responsible for the data

Each organisation that uses DigiVasool owns the borrower and loan records it enters and decides who in its team can see them. DigiVasool provides the software and keeps every organisation's data separate from every other organisation's. If you are a borrower, the lender who recorded your loan is the controller of your loan information.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your name and mobile number (used to sign in with a one-time password).
  • Borrower records entered by a lender: name, mobile numbers, address, area, Aadhaar number, photograph, signature, guarantor details, loan terms, payments, receipts and payment proofs.
  • Business records: expenses, capital entries, staff list, reminders and an audit log of actions taken in the app.
  • Technical data: basic request logs (time, endpoint) needed to run and secure the service. We do not log one-time passwords, Aadhaar numbers or access tokens.

How the information is used

  • To sign you in and show you only your own organisation's data.
  • To record loans and collections, produce receipts and reports, and send reminders or one-time passwords by SMS.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Only members of the same organisation, according to their role (owner, admin or collector). Collectors see only the borrowers assigned to them.
  • A borrower who signs in sees only their own loan.
  • Service providers that help us operate the app: our database host and an SMS gateway (to deliver one-time passwords). They process data only to provide that service.

Security

  • Every record belongs to exactly one organisation, enforced by the application and again by the database (row-level security).
  • Uploaded files are private and are served only to signed-in members of the owning organisation or through short-lived signed links.
  • Connections use HTTPS. Sessions expire and can be ended from the app.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept while the organisation uses the app. Anyone can remove their own access from Account → Delete my account. An organisation owner can lock and delete the whole organisation from Account → Delete organisation; the data is erased after a 30-day grace period. See how to delete your account. Financial records that a lender is legally required to keep may be retained by that lender.

Children

DigiVasool is not intended for anyone under 18.
