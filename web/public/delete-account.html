Delete your account

How to remove your access or your whole organisation

Admins and collectors

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Profile / Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your access to the organisation ends immediately. The loans and payments you recorded stay with the organisation they belong to.

Organisation owners

  1. Sign in and open More → Profile.
  2. Tap Delete organisation, type the organisation name and confirm.

The organisation is locked straight away — nobody can sign in. Its data is kept for 30 days so it can be restored if this was a mistake, then permanently erased. An owner who wants to leave without deleting the organisation can first hand ownership to another admin (Staff → Make owner).

Borrowers

Your loan record belongs to the lender who created it. To correct or delete your information, ask that lender, or write to us at [add your support email before launch] and tell us the lender’s name and your registered mobile number.

Can’t sign in?

Write to [add your support email before launch] from any address and tell us the mobile number on the account. We will verify it before acting.
